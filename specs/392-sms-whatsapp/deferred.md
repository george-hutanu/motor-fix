# Deferred — 392-sms-whatsapp

- MEDIUM (code-reviewer): `libs/domain/src/notifications/notifications.service.ts` `fallBack` (and `notify`, pre-existing) writes a `queued` row and then adds its job; if Redis refuses the add, the row stays `queued` with no job and nothing re-queues it. A sweeper that re-adds `send-<id>` for stale `queued` rows (idempotent by job id) would close it for every channel. — Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3f0607bff0d281c1b564c357379131d1
- MEDIUM (code-reviewer): `libs/domain/src/notifications/notifications.processor.ts` `sendSms` is at-least-once: Brevo accepts the SMS, then the `sent` write fails or the worker dies, and the retry sends and counts a second SMS; a timeout after Brevo accepted gives back an SMS that went. Same semantics as e-mail today; closing it needs a `sending` state recorded before the call. — Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3f0607bff0d28100bcb4d12439ca1fc4
- LOW (spec-reviewer): `libs/domain/src/notifications/notifications.service.ts` `fallBack` writes the next channel from the failed row alone, without the garage switch or verified-phone check `build` applies. Not reachable today (SMS is driver-only; the phone was verified when the SMS was built); the story that adds a staff SMS or removes a phone should route fallbacks through `outsideChannels`. — Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3f0607bff0d281f49b75c6d4cbafac66

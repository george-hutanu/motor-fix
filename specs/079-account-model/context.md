## Decisions

- One account holds several roles; the role used last (`ACCOUNT.last_role`) opens after sign-in — [ST-79 Build brief; Feature MF-6 final rule 7; ST-78 Done] (2026-10-03/04, confidence: high)
  - superseded by: the story's original criterion "The role is driver, garage, mechanic or admin" (2026-10-03, same page, marked Superseded)
- Role keys: `driver`, `garage` (owner, GARAGE_MEMBER role `owner`), `receptionist`, `mechanic`, `admin`. One `admin` role at launch, same rights for every admin (X21, A39). At most one owner membership per account (X20b), by a unique index (proposed) — [ST-79 Rules and validation] (2026-10-04, high)
- Mechanic: belongs to one garage (`MECHANIC.garage_id`), three permissions `can_move_bookings`, `can_answer_quotes`, `can_record_final_price` default false (R2). At launch a mechanic lands on a limited garage dashboard (W01) — [ST-79 Build brief; MF-6 rule 8] (2026-10-04, high)
  - superseded by: "A mechanic cannot open any view of the garage dashboard" (ST-79 criteria and MF-6 criteria, 2026-10-03)
- Landing: driver `/app/driver`; garage owner `/app/garage`; receptionist `/app/garage` without settings, prices or team (W10); mechanic `/app/garage` limited (W01); admin `/app/admin` — [MF-6 Build brief rule 8; ST-82 Rules] (2026-10-03, high)
- Another person's resource answers 404, not 403; 401 with code `sign_in_required` when not signed in; `account_suspended` for a suspended account; the policy runs in each use case, not only the controller — [ST-79 States and errors, Rules; A31] (2026-10-04, high; A31 is Proposed, ST-79 marks it *(proposed)*)
- Error shape: RFC 9457 problem details (`application/problem+json`) with a stable lower-snake-case `code` (A28, A42, both Proposed) — [Architecture decisions; Architecture > For build agents] (2026-10-03, medium-high)
- Prisma for data access and migrations (A6, Proposed; "Technology stack": Prisma with its migrations). REST under `/api/v1`, DTOs with class-validator in a shared contracts lib, OpenAPI-generated Angular client (A4) — [Technology stack; Architecture decisions] (2026-10-03, medium-high)
- Passwords: argon2id (ACCOUNT_IDENTITY `password_hash`). Access token lives 15 minutes, kept in memory; refresh token is an httpOnly cookie, rotated at every use, reuse closes the family. Own auth module (A11), no hosted identity service. **No JWT library, signing algorithm or token claims are named anywhere in the space I read** — [Security page "Stolen sign-in"; Technology stack "Sign-in"; ST-82 Rules] (2026-10-03, high that nothing is fixed)
- Events through a transactional outbox, carried by Redis (A7); audit entry and OUTBOX_EVENT row are written in the same transaction as the change — [Architecture decisions; Architecture > Data conventions] (2026-10-03, high)
- `ACCOUNT.last_role` is a view preference and is not audited; role added/removed and status changes are audited with actor, role, old and new value (A27) — [ST-79 Data; ST-394] (2026-10-04, high)
- Any call also carries `via_assistant` and `assistant_grant_id` when an AI assistant acts; "the policy accepts the assistant context now, so EP-16 adds no second rights system (proposed)" — [ST-79 Rules] (2026-10-04, medium)
- The capabilities matrix is "one table in code, in `libs/auth`, with a test for every 'may not' (proposed)" — [ST-79 Rules] (2026-10-04, medium)

## Constraints

- **Capabilities by role (Security page, last edited 2026-10-03T19:08Z, "current as of 2026-10-03").** The page is one row per capability with columns Visitor, Driver, Garage owner, Receptionist, Mechanic, MotorFix admin, AI assistant. Read per role, in its own words ("Own" = only their own; a name in code is a mechanic permission; "—" = may not). Every call checks role and ownership; a feature the garage switched off is refused for everyone at that garage.
  - **Driver** — may: search/open public pages; send a quote request to 1–5 garages and cancel it (Own); accept a quote (Own); propose/accept a new time (Own, max 2 moves, never within 2 h); cancel a booking (Own, late if within 2 h); one message per request (Own); see the car's number plate (Own); see the car history (Own); update a repair they logged, and delete it (Own); write a review within 60 days, edit/delete within 14, appeal once (Own); log repairs, sell a car with its history (Own); save a garage (Own); report a garage; report the garage's reply to their own review; review without a MotorFix job, only while "reviews only after a confirmed job" is off; audit history = short version for own car's jobs; delete own account; contact support. May not (—): send/change/withdraw a quote, confirm a booking, no-show, move job stages, record the final price, unlock a job, send photos/video, see a driver's phone, day sheets, profile/prices/team/switches, review replies, mute, garage figures, any admin item, odometer, correct final price, turn down a move, change a booking's mechanic/lift, block time.
  - **Garage owner** — may: public pages; send/change/withdraw a quote; confirm a booking; propose/accept a new time; cancel a booking with a reason; record a no-show; reply to a request message; move a job through its stages and change the estimated finish; record the final price at handover; unlock a job someone else locked (owner only); send photos, clips, live video, remove one; see the driver's phone "once a quote is accepted"; see the plate "while working on the car"; see the car history (with the driver's consent for that booking); update a repair that names the garage; day sheets; profile, brands, payment methods, courtesy car, closed days, facility requests; price list; feature switches, team, invites, mechanic cards and permissions; reply to and report a review; mute new request messages; audit history for the own garage; garage figures; upload a yearly document; record the odometer; correct the final price within 48 h; turn down a move request; change a booking's mechanic/lift and see the whole schedule; block time; delete own account only through support (garage closed first); contact support. May not: send a quote request, accept a quote, write a review, log repairs, save a garage, any admin item.
  - **Receptionist** — may: public pages; send/change/withdraw a quote; confirm a booking; propose/accept a new time; cancel a booking; record a no-show; reply to a message; record the final price at handover and the odometer at reception; see the driver's phone, the plate while the garage works on the car, the shared car history; day sheets; mute request messages for themselves; garage figures; audit history for the own garage; correct the final price within 48 h (proposed); turn down a move request; change a booking's mechanic/lift and see the whole schedule; block time; delete own account (leaves the team); contact support. May not (decided 2026-10-03, W10): move job stages or steps, change the estimated finish, send photos or video, reply to or report reviews, profile/prices/switches/team, unlock a job, update a driver-logged repair, any driver-only or admin item.
  - **Mechanic** — may: public pages; send/change/withdraw a quote only with `can_answer_quotes`; propose/accept a new time only with `can_move_bookings`; record a no-show on their booking; reply to drivers' messages when `can_answer_quotes`; move stages and change finish time on own jobs; record the final price only with `can_record_final_price`; send photos/clips/live on own jobs; see the plate on own jobs and own day sheet; see the shared car history on own bookings; own sheet; own mechanic card (photo, bio, languages, hide, search-engine switch); odometer if they are the job's mechanic; correct final price with `can_record_final_price` (proposed); turn down a move request with `can_move_bookings`; change a booking's mechanic/lift with `can_move_bookings`, without it only own bookings (proposed); audit history for the own garage; delete own account (leaves the team, reviews stay). May not: confirm a booking or a driver's new time (W11), cancel a booking, ever see the driver's phone ("Never"), unlock a job, profile/prices/switches/team, review replies, mute, garage figures, block time (proposed), any driver-only or admin item.
  - **MotorFix admin** — may: public pages; see the driver's phone, the plate and the car history; update a driver-logged repair ("every opening logged"); audit history "All"; garage figures "All"; verification (any admin: open, record checks, approve with a note, reject, ask for more, reopen); suspend/restore a garage, record the monthly RAR re-check; decide a reported review within 48 h; decide a review appeal within 48 h (not the admin who removed it); remove a facility, approve adding it back, approve a catalogue job; platform rules, warning limits, maintenance mode, copy voices (only "reviews only after a confirmed job" needs a second admin, W13); the switches that turn off manual approval and the RAR check, test environments only; decide on garage and review reports; answer support; accept/refuse a yearly document. May not: everything garage-side and driver-side (quotes, bookings, jobs, price list, team, reviews, saved garages, own-account deletion).
  - **Rights that depend on a permission**: the three `can_*` names above are the only mechanic permissions. Feature switches (team and mechanics, schedule by lift, live photos and video, day sheets, WhatsApp) refuse for everyone at that garage; GARAGE_FEATURE arrives with EP-2, until then every feature counts as on (ST-79).
  - **Customer data**: mechanic sees first name and car, never the phone, the plate only on own jobs and own day sheet; receptionist sees phone, plate and shared car history (ST-79 Rules, W10, W11).
  - **Account-level rights on the feature page** (MF-6 Permissions, 2026-10-03): only a garage owner may invite a mechanic or receptionist (own garage); creating an admin account is "MotorFix only, by a protected script (proposed)"; suspending, restoring or deleting another account is admin-only (MF-46); switching roles is "Own, with 2+ roles".

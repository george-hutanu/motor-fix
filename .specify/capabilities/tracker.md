# Capability: Tracker

How tasks, epics and their stages are tracked next to the code: a user-owned GitHub Project of george-hutanu linked to motor-fix, with the fields the Notion tracker had (Status, Priority, Work type, Epic, Ready to work, the lifecycle dates, Story points, Iteration), its views, issue forms per type, labels and milestones, epics as parent issues with their stories as sub-issues, Blocked by as issue dependencies, and the one-way import that brought every Notion story and epic over. Product documentation stays in Notion. The requirements arrive with ST-1017 (`1017-github-project-tracker`, slice 1) at its archive; the adapter that writes every lifecycle event there and the switch-off of the Notion writes are its later slices.
